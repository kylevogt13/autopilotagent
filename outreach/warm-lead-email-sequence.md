## Sequence Notes

- **Send timing:** Email 1 on Tuesday/Wednesday AM. Email 2 three days later. Email 3 four days after that.
- **Personalization required:** Research each prospect's recent transactions, area, and brokerage before sending Email 1. Reference specifics.
- **Response handling:** Any reply (positive or negative) exits the sequence. Positive replies get a same-day calendar link. Negative replies get a gracious thank-you.
- **Follow-up after sequence:** If no reply after Email 3, wait 30 days, then re-enter with a new angle (case study, seasonal hook, or referral ask).
