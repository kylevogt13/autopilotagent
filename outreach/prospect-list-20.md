# Autopilot Agent — Qualified Prospect List (20 Targets)

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):**
- Independent CA real estate agent or small team lead (1-3 agents)
- 10+ transactions/year (ideally 15-30 — busy enough to need help, not so big they have full staff)
- Located in major CA metros: Sacramento, Bay Area, Los Angeles, San Diego, Orange County, Inland Empire
- Active on social media (indicates tech comfort and content needs)
- Not already using a full-service AI or VA solution

**Qualification Criteria:**
- ✅ Transaction volume: 10+/year
- ✅ Market: California
- ✅ Team size: Solo or small team (1-3)
- ✅ Pain signals: High volume, visible content gaps, slow lead response
- ✅ Reachable: Email or LinkedIn available

---

## Research Sources for Building This List

1. **Zillow Agent Finder** — Filter by CA metros, sort by recent sales, look for agents with 10-30 transactions
2. **Realtor.com Agent Directory** — Similar filtering, cross-reference with Zillow data
3. **MLS records** — Public transaction data for CA counties (Sacramento, LA, SD, OC)
4. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — Search "real estate agent" + California, filter by activity level
5. **Facebook Groups** — Lab Coat Agents, Real Estate Agent groups, Sacramento RE groups
6. **Google Reviews** — Agents with high review counts tend to have high transaction volume
7. **Local RE publications** — Sacramento Business Journal, LA Business Journal top agent lists

## Prospect Research Process (per prospect)

1. Find agent via Zillow/Realtor.com/MLS — confirm 10+ annual transactions
2. Check their website and social media — note content quality and frequency
3. Find email (website, brokerage page, or Hunter.io) and LinkedIn profile
4. Note any pain signals: inconsistent posting, slow response times, no virtual assistant mentioned
5. Personalize Email 1 from the warm lead sequence with their specific details
6. Add to pipeline tracker with status "Researched — Ready for Outreach"
